RiskOpt Phase 3 — Risk Intelligence (wired)

Built from the stable Phase 2 report-export baseline.

What is wired:
1. Website assessment remains passive and evidence-based.
2. Observed findings are passed through lib/risk-interpreter.ts.
3. The interpreter translates findings into business-language risks and maps them to existing controls.
4. Website scan results now show a visible RiskOpt Intelligence section before budget selection.
5. When the user creates the website-based organization, the interpreted risks are stored in the organization model so Risk Explorer / Overview / Optimizer can use them.

What is intentionally NOT added yet:
- No external LLM/API key.
- No exploit execution or intrusive scanning.
- No redesign of existing product sections.
- No changes to the working report export implementation.
